# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Edward, eleven, checks in at home on a phone or tablet. His parents write the pack list, review activities, approve rewards, and record payouts on desktop. Source: docs/loadout-spec.md sections 1 and 6.

## Product Purpose

Make the daily pack check reliable and give the family a clear record of activities, chores, rewards, and money. Chore earnings pay weekly, half cash and half savings. Time spent on activities earns redeemable XP.

## Brand Personality

Clear, encouraging, practical. Preserve the established game HUD for Edward and quiet light parent workspace.

## Anti-references

No toy-like presentation, collectible characters, emojis, or gradients. These choices are documented in the existing build spec.

## Design Principles

- Keep the pack check easy to find.
- Separate earned money from XP available for rewards.
- Record payouts explicitly; never imply a transfer happened automatically.
- Preserve history and explain corrections.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve the existing responsive layout, native controls, visible focus and text status labels. Edward's touch controls should be at least 44px, as specified in the build spec.
